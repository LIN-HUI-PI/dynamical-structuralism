What This Is

A research programme exploring how invisible structural changes emerge before failure, how intervention windows can be preserved, and how human agency can remain sovereign within increasingly complex human–AI systems.

The theoretical foundation is Dynamical Structuralism (DS) — a philosophical and engineering framework proposing that structure emerges from tension dynamics, and that governance must operate at the level of geometry, not just behavior.

Building on this foundation, the programme develops the LIN System — a Human–AI–Physical Sovereign Governance Architecture — and translates its principles into an executable legal framework through IMLAIS.
